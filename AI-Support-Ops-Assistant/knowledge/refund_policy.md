# Refund Policy

## Purpose
Reference policy for handling billing and refund-related support tickets.

## Eligibility
- Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original charge.
- Duplicate charges (the same subscription or invoice billed more than once)
  are eligible for a full refund regardless of the 30-day window.
- Annual subscriptions cancelled within 14 days of purchase qualify for a
  full refund; after 14 days, refunds are prorated for unused months.
- Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing cycle has
  started, except in cases of a billing error (e.g., duplicate or incorrect
  charge amount).

## Non-Refundable Cases
- Usage-based add-on charges already consumed.
- Accounts suspended or terminated for a Terms of Service violation.
- Refund requests made more than 90 days after the charge date.

## Process
1. Verify the charge in the billing system (amount, date, subscription plan).
2. Confirm the reason matches one of the eligibility cases above.
3. If eligible, issue the refund via the original payment method within
   5-7 business days.
4. If not eligible, respond to the customer with the specific reason and
   any partial-credit alternatives available.

## Escalation
Escalate to Finance Support leadership when:
- The refund amount exceeds $500.
- The customer disputes a decision after an initial denial.
- The charge pattern suggests a billing system defect affecting multiple
  customers (in which case also notify Technical Support).
